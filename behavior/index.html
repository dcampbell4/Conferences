Welcome 👋

  1. Upload your roster below (Excel or CSV), or paste names.
  2. Pick a class from the buttons at the top.
  3. On the Log tab, tap a coloured button to log a CON flag. Press and hold to log INT. Tap ★ for a positive moment.
  4. Presenting? Click ⧉ Pop-out widget for a mini-window that floats over your slides.

🔒 Everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use Backup below to move data between devices.
